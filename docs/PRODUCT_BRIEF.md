# Product Brief: Corporate Technology Intelligence

## Customer problem

Large companies need to identify credible technology partners and understand competitor activity. Raw company lists are not enough: buyers need evidence-backed relationships, clear legal-entity identity, relationship stage, recency and confidence.

## Target users

- Corporate strategy and innovation teams
- Technology scouting and R&D teams
- Procurement and vendor-management teams
- Corporate venture and partnership teams
- Market-intelligence providers

## Core product

A searchable catalogue of enterprise–technology provider relationships with separate legal-entity records, relationship type and lifecycle stage, evidence source and date, confidence and completeness indicators, human-review status, correction workflow and API-ready structured output.

## MVP boundary

The first MVP should focus on one segment (for example industrial automation) and a manageable set of high-value relationship types. It should not attempt to cover every company, industry and signal from day one.

## Validation plan

1. Interview 8–12 existing customers or prospects about decisions they make and data they currently buy.
2. Build a manually checked sample for one sector and one use case.
3. Test search and evidence display with 3–5 design partners.
4. Offer 2–3 paid pilots with explicit scope, usage rights and success criteria.
5. Measure willingness to pay, repeat usage, record acceptance rate, correction rate, freshness and cost per accepted record.

## Commercial metrics

- Paid pilot conversion rate
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR)
- Gross margin by data product
- Cost per validated/accepted relationship
- Customer usage and retention
- Coverage and freshness of priority entities
- Correction and dispute rate

## Key risks

- Weak or unclear source licensing
- Entity-resolution mistakes and false merges
- Stale or unsupported claims
- Inconsistent definitions of pilot, partnership and implementation
- GDPR or other privacy obligations where personal data is involved
- High supplier/API costs without willingness to pay

## Product principle

Sell a verified decision advantage, not raw volume. Each customer-facing fact should retain provenance, recency, scope and a clear uncertainty signal.
